## Metric definitions

- nDCG@10: exponential gain `2^grade - 1`, log2 discount, ideal ranking from all labels.
- MRR@10: reciprocal rank of the first relevant passage in the top 10, else 0.
- Recall@k: share of labelled passages in the top k. P@5: relevant passages in the top 5 divided by 5.
- Grades: only nDCG@10 uses them. MRR, Recall and P@5 count a grade-1 (partial) label exactly like a grade-2 (direct answer) label.
- Passages: a section chunk is credited to its section; a fixed window to every section it overlaps by at least 100 characters or contains completely. Each credited passage takes its own rank.
- Reported metrics: ndcg@10, mrr@10, recall@5, recall@10, precision@5; compared metrics: ndcg@10, mrr@10, recall@10.
